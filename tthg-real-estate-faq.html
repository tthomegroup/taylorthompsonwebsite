What is my home worth?

Your home’s value depends on location, square footage, lot size, condition, upgrades, layout, school district, current inventory, buyer demand, and recent comparable sales. Taylor Thompson Home Group can prepare a customized home value analysis. Please share the property address to get started.

How do I start the process of selling my home?

Start by scheduling a consultation with Taylor Thompson Home Group. The team will review your property, discuss your goals, analyze comparable sales, recommend a pricing strategy, and outline the best plan to prepare, market, and negotiate your home sale.

What information do you need to value my home?

To prepare a helpful home value estimate, Taylor Thompson Home Group needs the property address, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, approximate square footage if known, major upgrades, condition, special features, and your ideal selling timeline.

How long does it take to sell a home?

Timing depends on price, location, condition, inventory, buyer demand, interest rates, and marketing strategy. Homes that are priced correctly and marketed well generally attract more attention and stronger offers.

Should I sell my home now or wait?

The right timing depends on your personal goals, equity, next move, market conditions, and current buyer demand. Taylor Thompson Home Group can help you compare your options and decide whether selling now makes sense.

What costs should I expect when selling a home?

Potential seller costs may include real estate commissions, title fees, escrow fees, transfer taxes, repairs, seller credits, home warranty, mortgage payoff, and any negotiated closing costs. Taylor Thompson Home Group can create a custom net sheet for your property.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

Not always. Some repairs can improve value and buyer confidence, while others may not provide a strong return. Taylor Thompson Home Group can walk through the home and help prioritize repairs that make financial sense.

Should I remodel before selling?

Usually, major remodels are not necessary before selling. Strategic, cost-effective improvements such as paint, landscaping, lighting, deep cleaning, repairs, and staging often provide a better return than expensive renovations.

Should I stage my home before selling?

Staging can help buyers emotionally connect with the home and understand how spaces function. Taylor Thompson Home Group can advise whether staging, light styling, or simple decluttering is the best fit for your home.

What should I do to prepare my house for listing photos?

Declutter, deep clean, remove personal items, open window coverings, touch up paint if needed, improve curb appeal, hide cords and trash cans, clear counters, and make each room feel bright, clean, and welcoming.

Should I price high and leave room to negotiate?

Overpricing can reduce buyer interest, limit showings, and cause a home to sit longer. A strong pricing strategy is based on current competition, recent comparable sales, condition, and buyer demand.

What is a comparative market analysis?

A comparative market analysis, or CMA, compares your home to similar homes that recently sold, are pending, or are currently active. It helps determine a realistic and strategic listing price.

What happens after I accept an offer?

After accepting an offer, the transaction typically moves into escrow. The process may include inspections, appraisal, buyer loan approval, disclosure review, repair negotiations, signing closing documents, and final closing.

Home Buying FAQ

Questions about pre-approval, offers, inspections, escrow, closing costs, contingencies, and finding the right home.

Do I need to be pre-approved before touring homes?

Yes. A pre-approval helps determine your buying power and shows sellers you are serious. Many sellers expect buyers to be pre-approved before accepting an offer.

Can Taylor Thompson Home Group recommend lenders?

Yes. Taylor Thompson Home Group can connect buyers with trusted local lending partners who can explain loan options, down payments, closing costs, and pre-approval.

How much house can I afford?

Affordability depends on income, debts, credit score, down payment, interest rate, loan program, taxes, insurance, and comfort level with monthly payment. A lender can provide a personalized pre-approval.

How much down payment do I need?

Down payment requirements vary by loan type. Some buyers qualify for low down payment programs, while others choose larger down payments. A lender can explain your specific options.

Can I buy a home with less than 20% down?

Yes. Many buyers purchase homes with less than 20% down depending on loan program, credit profile, and lender guidelines.

What credit score do I need to buy a home?

Credit score requirements vary by lender and loan program. A lender can review your situation and help you understand which loan options may fit.

What are closing costs for buyers?

Buyer closing costs may include lender fees, escrow fees, title fees, appraisal, inspections, prepaid taxes, homeowner’s insurance, and other loan-related costs.

What is earnest money?

Earnest money is a good-faith deposit made after an offer is accepted. It shows the seller the buyer is serious and is typically applied toward the buyer’s closing costs or down payment.

What happens after my offer is accepted?

After your offer is accepted, escrow opens. The buyer typically completes inspections, reviews disclosures, works through appraisal and loan approval, signs closing documents, and then closes escrow.

How long does it take to buy a home?

Most financed home purchases close in about 21 to 45 days, depending on the loan, appraisal, inspections, negotiations, and escrow timeline.

Can I buy a home before selling my current home?

Possibly. Options may include bridge financing, a home equity line, a contingent offer, a rent-back, or selling first and then buying. Taylor Thompson Home Group can help evaluate options.

What is a buyer consultation?

A buyer consultation is a meeting where Taylor Thompson Home Group reviews your goals, budget, timeline, desired locations, home features, financing, offer strategy, and the buying process.

Do you help first-time homebuyers?

Yes. Taylor Thompson Home Group helps first-time buyers understand financing, inspections, contingencies, contracts, negotiations, timelines, and the steps to becoming a homeowner.

Do I need a Realtor to buy a home?

Having a Realtor helps protect your interests, navigate contracts, analyze value, negotiate terms, coordinate inspections, review disclosures, and manage deadlines.

What is a buyer representation agreement?

A buyer representation agreement outlines the relationship between a buyer and their real estate agent, including representation, expectations, and terms of service.

Why do buyers need to sign paperwork before showings?

California real estate rules and brokerage policies may require certain buyer representation or disclosure forms before private showings. Taylor Thompson Home Group can explain the form clearly before you sign.

Can you help me find homes online?

Yes. Taylor Thompson Home Group can help set up a customized home search based on your preferred cities, budget, bedrooms, bathrooms, lot size, school areas, and must-have features.

Can you help me buy a home from a builder?

Yes. Taylor Thompson Home Group helps buyers with new construction, builder incentives, lot selection, upgrade decisions, contract questions, inspections, and negotiation strategy.

Can I tour homes this weekend?

Possibly. To schedule showings, Taylor Thompson Home Group will need your name, phone number, email, preferred homes, availability, and pre-approval status.

How do I make a strong offer?

A strong offer depends on price, terms, contingencies, closing timeline, earnest money, financing strength, seller priorities, and market competition. Taylor Thompson Home Group helps buyers build competitive offers.

Should I waive inspections to win a house?

Waiving inspections carries risk. Taylor Thompson Home Group helps buyers understand options and risks before deciding how to structure an offer.

What inspections should I consider?

Common inspections may include a general home inspection, pest inspection, roof inspection, sewer inspection, pool inspection, HVAC inspection, chimney inspection, and septic or well inspections when applicable.

What is an appraisal?

An appraisal is a lender-required opinion of value that helps confirm whether the property supports the loan amount.

What if the appraisal comes in low?

If an appraisal comes in low, options may include renegotiating price, increasing down payment, challenging the appraisal, splitting the difference, or canceling if protected by contract terms.

When do I get the keys?

Buyers typically receive keys after the transaction records with the county and escrow confirms closing.

What is pre-approval?

Pre-approval is a lender’s review of your financial information to estimate how much you may be able to borrow for a home purchase.

What is the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

Pre-qualification is usually a lighter estimate, while pre-approval typically includes a more detailed review of income, credit, assets, and debts.

Money

Financing FAQ

Common financing questions about lenders, pre-approval, loan types, credits, rate buy-downs, and escrow mistakes to avoid.

What loan types are available?

Common loan types include conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, and specialized programs. A lender can recommend options based on your situation.

Can I buy a home with VA financing?

Many eligible veterans and service members can purchase with VA financing. Taylor Thompson Home Group can help connect you with a lender experienced in VA loans.

Can I use FHA financing?

Many buyers use FHA financing, especially if they need flexible down payment or credit options. A lender can confirm eligibility.

What is a rate buy-down?

A rate buy-down uses funds to reduce the buyer’s interest rate for a temporary or permanent period, depending on the structure.

Can sellers help pay buyer closing costs?

Sometimes. Seller credits depend on the offer, loan rules, market conditions, and negotiation strategy.

What should I avoid during escrow?

Avoid opening new credit, making large purchases, changing jobs, missing payments, moving large unexplained funds, or doing anything that could affect loan approval.

How soon should I talk to a lender?

Buyers should talk to a lender early, even if they are months away from buying. Early planning helps identify budget, credit improvements, and savings goals.
