As the seasons change, your home needs a little attention too.

Fall is a great time to take care of those maintenance tasks you've been putting off before cooler weather arrives. A little preparation now can help prevent bigger problems later, keep your home comfortable, and make sure everything is working properly when you need it most.

Here are five things to add to your fall home maintenance checklist.

## 1. Check Your Heating System

### Get ahead of chilly nights.

Before you really need your heat, take a few minutes to make sure your heating system is ready for the season.

Have your HVAC system inspected if needed, replace your filters, and make sure everything is running efficiently. Taking care of these things early can help you avoid discovering a problem on the first cold night of the season.

It's also a good time to make sure your vents aren't blocked and that your home is heating evenly.

## 2. Clean Out Your Gutters

### Keep water moving where it should.

Leaves and debris can quickly build up in your gutters during the fall.

Cleaning them out and checking your downspouts can help make sure rainwater is able to drain properly instead of collecting around your roof or foundation.

While you're up there, take a look for any loose sections, damage, or areas where water isn't flowing properly.

A little gutter maintenance can go a long way toward helping prevent water damage.

## 3. Freshen Up Your Yard

### A little yard work goes a long way.

Fall is a great time to get your landscaping in shape.

Trim overgrown trees and shrubs, clear away fallen leaves, and take a look around your yard for anything that needs attention.

Pay particular attention to trees near your home, roof, driveway, or power lines. Removing problematic branches before storms or strong winds can help protect your property.

And let's be honest, a clean and maintained yard makes your home look better too. 🍂🏡

## 4. Seal Up Drafty Spots

### Keep the cozy in and the cold out.

Take a walk around your home and check your windows, doors, and other openings for gaps or worn weatherstripping.

Small drafts can make rooms feel colder and may cause your heating system to work harder than necessary.

Replacing worn weatherstripping or sealing small gaps can be a simple way to make your home more comfortable and energy efficient.

Don't forget to check doors leading to garages and other areas that may not be heated.

## 5. Give Your Fireplace Some Attention

### Before you light that first fire...

If your home has a fireplace or chimney, fall is a good time to make sure it's ready to use.

Have your fireplace and chimney inspected and cleaned as needed, particularly if you use your fireplace regularly.

It's a simple maintenance step that can help keep your home safer throughout the season.

And once everything is ready, you can actually enjoy that cozy first fire without wondering if you forgot something. 🔥

## A Little Fall Maintenance Now Can Save You Trouble Later

Home maintenance isn't always the most exciting item on your to do list, but staying ahead of small issues can make homeownership a lot easier.

Before the season gets too busy, take a weekend to walk around your home and look for anything that needs attention.

Check your heating system.

Clean those gutters.

Tidy up the yard.

Seal those drafts.

And make sure your fireplace is ready for the season.

Five simple tasks can help your home feel more comfortable and prepared for the months ahead.

**Your future self will thank you. 🍂🏡**
