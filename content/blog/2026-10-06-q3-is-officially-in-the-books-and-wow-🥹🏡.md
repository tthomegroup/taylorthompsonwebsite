Q3 is officially wrapped, and looking back at the numbers, I’m feeling incredibly grateful.

**55 families served. $28.6 million in sales volume. And we’re not done yet.**

But honestly, the numbers are only part of the story.

Behind every transaction is a real person, a real family, a big decision, and a new chapter. It’s someone who trusted us to guide them through one of the biggest financial and personal decisions they’ll make. **That is the part I’ll never take for granted.** ❤️

## 55 Families. 55 Stories.

Our year to date numbers include **33 buyers and 22 sellers**, for a total of **55 families served so far this year.**

From helping someone purchase their first home to helping a family sell and move into their next chapter, every transaction looks a little different.

And that’s exactly what makes this business so rewarding.

## $28.6 Million in Sales Volume

We’re incredibly proud to have reached **$28,603,779 in year to date sales volume** through Q3.

That number represents a lot of late nights, negotiations, showings, inspections, phone calls, paperwork, problem solving, and probably more coffee than I care to admit. 😂

But most importantly, it represents the trust our clients have placed in us.

## Serving Families Across the Central Valley

Our clients aren't all in one city, and neither is our reach.

We’re proud to serve families throughout the Central Valley, including:

**Modesto, Turlock, Ceres, Stockton, Ripon, Hughson, Denair, Tracy, Manteca, Lathrop, Atwater, Escalon, Lodi, Oakdale, Patterson, Riverbank, Salida, Waterford, Livingston, and Merced.**

Whether you're moving across town or making a bigger move, we're here to help make the process feel a little less overwhelming and a lot more manageable.

## And We're Just Getting Started

We're heading into Q4 with **12 active and coming soon listings** and one BIG goal:

**Help 15 more families before the end of the year.** 🔥

So if buying or selling has been on your mind, this might be your sign to start the conversation.

And if you know someone who's been talking about making a move, we'd be honored to be the people you send them to.

## The Biggest Compliment You Can Give Us

One of the greatest compliments you can give our team isn't a review, a like, or even a referral.

It's **trusting us with the people you love.**

When you share our posts, recommend us to a friend, invite someone to one of our client events, leave us a review, or simply think of us when someone mentions real estate, it means more than you probably realize.

Our business is built on relationships, and we genuinely wouldn't be here without the people who continue to support us, cheer us on, and send their friends and family our way. ❤️

So from the bottom of our hearts, **thank you.**

Thank you to the 55 families who trusted us this year. Thank you to everyone who has referred someone, shared a post, left a review, attended an event, or simply followed along.

**Q4, LET'S GOOOOOO! 🏡🔥**
