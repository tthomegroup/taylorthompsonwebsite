Modesto has a vibrant food scene that’s perfect for anyone who loves trying new dishes or just grabbing a great meal with friends and family. I’ve put together my top 5 favorite restaurants, all of which are personal favorites and must-visits if you’re in the area. You can also check out the original Instagram post here to see it in action!

### 1. Camp 4 Wine Café
The poke bowl at Camp 4 Wine Café is my absolute favorite. Fresh, flavorful, and perfectly balanced, it’s a must-try for anyone who loves a light yet satisfying meal. Their cozy café vibe makes it a great spot to relax, catch up with friends, or enjoy a quiet lunch.

### 2. Harvest Moon
Harvest Moon is where I go when I want comfort food with a creative twist. Seasonal dishes, locally sourced ingredients, and a welcoming atmosphere make it a favorite for casual dinners or special nights out.

### 3. Galleto Ristorante
Italian cuisine lovers will adore Galleto Ristorante. Fresh pasta, classic entrees, and a charming setting make it a go-to for date nights, family dinners, or any occasion where Italian flavors are calling.

### 4. Dewz Restaurant
Dewz is perfect for adventurous eaters. The menu is full of bold flavors and creative dishes that never fail to surprise me. It’s a casual, fun spot with something for everyone.

### 5. Skewers Kabob House
If you love Mediterranean cuisine, Skewers Kabob House is a must. The kabobs are fresh and flavorful, and the generous portions make it ideal for lunch or dinner with friends.
