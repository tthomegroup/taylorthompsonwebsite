Thanksgiving is a time to gather with the people we love, reflect on what we’re grateful for, and show up for our neighbors. 🧡

Whether you’re looking for a Thanksgiving meal, a way to donate, or a fun event to enjoy with family and friends, there are opportunities to connect and give back throughout the Central Valley this November.

Here are a few to put on your calendar!

**Thanksgiving at the Mission | Modesto**

📅 Wednesday, November 25 | 2–4:30 p.m.\
📍 1400 Yosemite Boulevard, Modesto

The Modesto Gospel Mission is hosting a Thanksgiving meal and offering free winter clothing to community members.

Want to help? The Mission also welcomes volunteers and donations of food and warm clothing. Its wish list includes items such as turkeys, stuffing, canned vegetables, coats, hats, gloves, and socks.

[Find event and donation details](https://www.mymission.org/thanksgiving)

**Thanksgiving Food Distribution | Merced**

📅 Monday, November 23 | Beginning at 8 a.m.\
📍 St. Patrick Church, 671 E. Yosemite Avenue, Merced

Catholic Charities is distributing turkeys and Thanksgiving food bags on a first-come, first-served basis.

This is a **drive-through-only distribution**. The event flyer directs those without a vehicle to Catholic Charities after 1 p.m. that day. For questions, call **209-383-2494**.

[View the 2026 distribution flyer](https://ccdof.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MER-Thanksgiving-Distribution-2026.pdf)

**Stuff the Bus Food Drive | Stockton**

📅 Friday, November 20 and Saturday, November 21 | 9 a.m.–5 p.m.\
📍 Food 4 Less, 789 W. Hammer Lane, Stockton

Help fill the bus and support the local food bank! Community members can donate frozen turkeys, hams, chickens, or canned food during the two-day drive.

This is a **donation collection**, not a food pickup event.

[Learn more about Stuff the Bus](https://sanjoaquinrtd.com/event/stuff-the-bus-2026/)

**Table of Plenty Food Drive | Merced**

📅 Donate by November 17\
📍 Yosemite Church, 2230 E. Yosemite Avenue, Merced

Yosemite Church is collecting food for Thanksgiving boxes to help local families. Its requested items include canned vegetables and cranberries, boxed stuffing, mashed potatoes, and gravy.

The church also lists turkey among the requested items. If you’d like to donate one, contact the church first to confirm how to drop off a perishable item.

[See the food drive details and wish list](https://yosemitechurch.com/calendar/2026/11/1/table-of-plenty)

**Turlock’s 52nd Annual Turkey Trot | Turlock**

📅 Saturday, November 21\
📍 Donnelly Park, 600 Pedras Road, Turlock

Looking for a way to get moving before Thanksgiving? Turlock’s annual Turkey Trot offers a one-mile run for children, a one-mile walk, and a two-mile run for older participants.

The city lists registration through **November 14**, so check the flyer soon if you’d like to participate. The flyer does not list a start time. For details, contact Turlock Recreation at **209-668-5594**.

[View the Turkey Trot flyer](https://new.turlock.ca.us/_pdf/recholidayevents.asp?id=14&n=1)

**Merced Turkey Trot | Merced**

📅 Saturday, November 21\
📍 Lake Yosemite County Park, 714 Lake Road, Merced

Enjoy a morning outdoors while supporting Merced County Toys for Tots! The kids’ 1K starts at **10:15 a.m.**, followed by the two-mile and five-mile events at **10:30 a.m.** Check-in begins at 9 a.m.

Participants can bring a new, unwrapped toy for a raffle ticket.

[Learn more about the Merced Turkey Trot](https://mercedrunningclub.com/home/running-events/tt/)
