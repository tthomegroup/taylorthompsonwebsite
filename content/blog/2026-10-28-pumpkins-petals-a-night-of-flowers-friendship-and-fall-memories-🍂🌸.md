Some nights are just too good not to look back on, and our Pumpkins & Petals event was definitely one of them! 🥹

We brought together a wonderful group of women for an evening filled with beautiful flowers, creative pumpkin arrangements, plenty of laughter, and the kind of conversations that make you wish the night didn't have to end.

There were flowers everywhere, pumpkins turned into gorgeous centerpieces, and so many sweet moments happening around the table. Everyone put their own touch on their arrangements, and seeing all the finished creations together was such a fun part of the night.

But honestly? The flowers were only part of what made the evening special.

## Flowers, Friendship, and a Little Fall Magic

There is something about getting together with other women, putting the busy parts of life on pause, and making time to enjoy the moment.

Between arranging flowers, sharing stories, taking pictures, and laughing together, the evening became about so much more than a fall craft. It was an opportunity to connect, meet new people, catch up with familiar faces, and create memories outside our usual routines.

And let's talk about those arrangements! The combination of soft pumpkins, colorful blooms, and beautiful greenery made every creation feel unique. Everyone left with something they made themselves, which made the night even more special.

These are the moments I want to remember. 🤎

## More Than an Event

One of my favorite things about hosting events like this is getting to create opportunities for people to come together.

Life gets busy. Between work, family, responsibilities, and everything else filling our calendars, it's easy to keep saying we'll make time for fun later.

But sometimes, all it takes is an evening with good people, a little creativity, and a reason to get out of the house.

That's what made Pumpkins & Petals so special. It gave everyone a chance to slow down, enjoy something different, and leave with a beautiful arrangement and memories to go with it.

As a Realtor, I love helping people find a place to call home, but I also love being part of the community beyond the real estate side of things. Creating opportunities for people to connect is one of the sweetest parts of what I get to do.

## A Huge Thank You to Everyone Who Joined Us

To every woman who came out and spent the evening with us, thank you! Your energy, creativity, and willingness to jump in made this event what it was.

A special thank you to Carli at [@cb_blooms209](https://www.instagram.com/cb_blooms209/) for being part of the floral magic and helping bring the vision to life. The arrangements were beautiful, and we loved seeing everyone's creativity come through.

## Same Time Next Year?

Already asking the important questions! 😂🥂

Pumpkins & Petals was such a special evening, and we're already looking forward to the possibility of doing it again next year. Maybe even bigger, with more flowers, more friends, and even more memories to make.

Until then, we're holding onto these photos and all the little moments that made the night so memorable.

Thank you again to everyone who made Pumpkins & Petals such a success. Here's to more nights spent creating, connecting, and making time for the people who make life extra sweet. 🌸🤎
