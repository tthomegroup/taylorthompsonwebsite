One of the best parts about living in Modesto is that there's always something fun happening nearby. Whether you're looking for an outdoor adventure, a budget friendly weekend activity, or a place where the kids can burn off some energy, the Central Valley offers plenty of family friendly options.

If you're searching for ideas for your next family outing, here are some of our favorite things to do around Modesto this month.

## Spend the Day at Graceada Park

Graceada Park has been a local favorite for generations.

Families can enjoy:

* Large playgrounds
* Open green spaces
* Walking paths
* Picnic areas
* Tennis courts
* Seasonal community events

It's the perfect place to pack a lunch and spend a relaxing afternoon outdoors.

## Cool Off at a Splash Pad

Nothing beats a splash pad on a warm Central Valley afternoon.

Several nearby parks offer safe places for younger children to cool off while parents relax nearby.

Bring sunscreen, towels, and plenty of water because the kids probably won't want to leave.

## Visit the Great Valley Museum

Located on the Modesto Junior College campus, the Great Valley Museum offers hands on exhibits that make learning fun.

Kids love exploring science, wildlife, fossils, and the planetarium while parents appreciate the educational experience.

It's a great option when you're looking for an indoor activity.

## Explore Downtown Modesto

Downtown Modesto is full of family friendly charm.

Spend an afternoon:

* Grabbing ice cream
* Visiting local coffee shops
* Shopping small businesses
* Admiring colorful murals
* Enjoying local events throughout the year

There's always something new to discover.

## Support Your Local Farmers Market

Farmers markets are a wonderful way to spend a Saturday morning.

Kids can enjoy fresh fruit, homemade treats, live music, and local vendors while parents shop for fresh produce and artisan goods.

It's also a great opportunity to support small businesses in the community.

## Take a Short Drive to Jacob Myers Park

Located in nearby Riverbank, Jacob Myers Park offers beautiful views of the Stanislaus River.

Families can enjoy:

* Walking trails
* Picnic tables
* Fishing
* Bird watching
* Open grassy areas

It's a peaceful escape just minutes from Modesto.

## Catch a Modesto Nuts Baseball Game

Nothing says summer quite like baseball.

Grab a hot dog, cheer on the home team, and enjoy a fun evening with the entire family.

The atmosphere makes it one of the best family outings in town.

## Plan a Backyard Picnic

Sometimes the best memories happen at home.

Pick up sandwiches from a local deli, grab fresh fruit from the farmers market, set up a blanket in the backyard, and enjoy an unplugged afternoon together.

Simple moments often become the ones kids remember most.

## Explore Local Bike Trails

The Virginia Corridor Trail is one of Modesto's favorite places for walking and biking.

Bring the kids, ride at your own pace, and enjoy some fresh air while getting a little exercise together.

## Check Out Community Events

Throughout the year, Modesto and the surrounding Central Valley communities host family friendly events including:

* Other concerts
* Seasonal festivals
* Holiday celebrations
* Movie nights in the park
* Food truck events
* Community fairs

There's almost always something happening nearby, making it easy to plan a fun weekend without traveling far.

## Why Families Love Living Around Modesto

One of the reasons so many families choose to call Modesto home is the variety of activities available close to home.

Whether your family enjoys parks, sports, community events, museums, or simply spending time outdoors, there's never a shortage of things to do.

The Central Valley offers a lifestyle that combines affordability, convenience, and a strong sense of community, making it a wonderful place to raise a family.
