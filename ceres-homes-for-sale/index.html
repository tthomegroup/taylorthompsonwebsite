Area Guide

Looking for a Home in Ceres?

Located in southern Stanislaus County, Ceres offers homebuyers a convenient location just minutes from Modesto while maintaining a strong community atmosphere. Buyers are attracted to Ceres for its relatively affordable home prices, family friendly neighborhoods, newer housing developments, and easy Highway 99 access. With a population of approximately 49,000 residents, Ceres continues to grow while remaining one of the Central Valley's most accessible housing markets.
