Should You Buy First or Sell First? A Guide for Central Valley Homeowners

One of the biggest questions homeowners face when planning a move is whether they should buy their next home first or sell their current home first. The answer is different for everyone, but understanding the pros and cons of each option can help you make the best decision for your situation.

Buying First

Buying before you sell can provide peace of mind because you already know where you are going. There is no need to worry about finding temporary housing or rushing to find your next home.

Benefits of buying first include:

  • More time to find the right home
  • Less pressure during your home search
  • A smoother transition for your family

However, buying first may require qualifying for two mortgage payments at the same time, depending on your financial situation.

Selling First

Selling first allows you to know exactly how much equity you have available for your next purchase. This often makes budgeting easier and can strengthen your position when shopping for your next home.

Benefits of selling first include:

  • Clear understanding of your available funds
  • Reduced financial risk
  • Potentially stronger negotiating position when buying

The challenge is that you may need temporary housing if you do not find your next home right away.

What About Contingencies?

Many homeowners choose a middle ground by making offers contingent on the sale of their current home. Depending on market conditions, this can be a great strategy that provides flexibility while minimizing risk.

The Central Valley Market Matters

The right approach often depends on local market conditions. Inventory levels, buyer demand, interest rates, and your specific goals all play a role in determining which option makes the most sense.

That is why having a plan before making a move is so important.
