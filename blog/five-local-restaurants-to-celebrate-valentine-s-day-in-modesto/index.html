Five Local Restaurants to Celebrate Valentine’s Day in Modesto

If you are planning a Valentine’s date this year, here are five local favorites with their locations and contact details to help you plan ahead.

Valentine’s Day is a chance to slow down, reconnect, and enjoy a great meal together. One of the things I love most about living in Modesto is how many locally owned restaurants create the perfect setting for a meaningful night out, whether you are celebrating something new or honoring years together.

Bistro 120

Bistro 120 is a beautiful choice for a romantic evening with an intimate atmosphere and thoughtfully prepared dishes. It feels special without being overly formal, making it perfect for lingering conversations.

Address 120 W Orangeburg Ave, Modesto, CA 95350 Phone 209 524 1200

Falcon’s Lair

Falcon’s Lair is a longtime Modesto favorite that blends a relaxed vibe with classic comfort food. It is ideal for couples who want Valentine’s Day to feel fun, familiar, and easygoing.

Address 1400 Standiford Ave, Modesto, CA 95350 Phone 209 523 4666

Ernie’s Food and Spirits

Ernie’s Food and Spirits offers a timeless steakhouse experience with warm service and quality dishes. It is a great option for couples who enjoy tradition, great food, and a cozy night out.

Address 834 W Roseburg Ave, Modesto, CA 95350 Phone 209 577 9711

Pietro’s Pizza

Valentine’s Day does not have to be fancy to be meaningful. Pietro’s Pizza is perfect for a laid back date night filled with nostalgia, shared plates, and easy conversation.

Address 1200 McHenry Ave, Modesto, CA 95350 Phone 209 576 9400

Camp 4 Wine Cafe

Camp 4 Wine Cafe is a go to for a cozy and modern Valentine’s celebration. With a great wine selection and a menu made for sharing, it is perfect for couples who enjoy a relaxed but thoughtful dining experience.

Address 1530 Oakdale Rd, Modesto, CA 95355 Phone 209 551 1700

Celebrate Valentine’s Day the Local Way

Choosing a local restaurant is a wonderful way to support small businesses while creating meaningful memories close to home. Whether you are planning something elegant, classic, or casual, Modesto offers so many great options for a special night out.
