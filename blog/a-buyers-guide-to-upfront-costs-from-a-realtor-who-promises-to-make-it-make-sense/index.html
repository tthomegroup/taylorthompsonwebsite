A Buyer's Guide to Upfront Costs (From a Realtor Who Promises to Make It Make Sense)

Buying your first home doesn't have to be confusing. Learn what upfront costs to expect, from your down payment to closing costs, in this easy to understand guide that makes the home buying process simple.

Let's play a quick game.

You find your dream home.

Your offer gets accepted.

You're already mentally picking paint colors, planning where the couch goes, and arguing over who gets the bigger closet.

Then someone says...

"You'll need to bring your funds to close."

Wait...

The what now?

If you're buying your first home, the upfront costs can feel like someone suddenly handed you a restaurant bill with seventeen extra charges you definitely didn't order.

The good news?

They're not random.

Let's break them down in plain English.

💰 The Down Payment

Let's start with the one everyone knows.

Your down payment is the amount you contribute toward the purchase price of your home.

Contrary to popular belief...

No, you don't automatically need 20%.

Many buyers qualify with much less depending on the type of loan they're using.

So if you've been waiting until you save enough to buy a small island...

Let's have a conversation first.

🤝 Earnest Money Deposit

Think of earnest money as your way of saying:

"I promise I'm serious."

It's a good faith deposit submitted after your offer is accepted.

The money doesn't disappear.

It typically goes toward your down payment or closing costs when everything is finalized.

📋 Home Inspection

This is where someone gets paid to crawl into places you'd never willingly enter.

Attics.

Crawl spaces.

Roofs.

Under the house.

They're looking for issues before they become your issues.

Could you skip it?

Technically.

Would I recommend it?

Absolutely not.

📏 The Appraisal

This isn't someone judging your decorating skills.

The lender hires an appraiser to determine whether the home is worth what you're paying.

Think of it as your bank asking,

"Are we all comfortable with this price?"

📝 Closing Costs

Ah yes.

The category that sounds mysterious because it includes several different services.

Closing costs may include things like:

  • Escrow services
  • Title insurance
  • Loan fees
  • Recording fees
  • Prepaid taxes
  • Homeowners insurance

No, they're not random surprise charges.

Yes, we'll explain every single one before you sign anything.

🏡 Moving Expenses

This is the part buyers often forget.

Boxes.

Moving truck.

Pizza for the friends you convinced to help.

New locks.

Curtains.

That giant sectional you suddenly need because the new living room is bigger.

It all adds up.

☕ The "Why Did Nobody Tell Me?" Fund

Every homeowner discovers something within the first month.

Maybe it's:

  • A garden hose.
  • A ladder.
  • Window coverings.
  • A rake.
  • A lawn mower.
  • More trips to Home Depot than you'd like to admit.

Welcome to homeownership.

You'll understand soon enough.

The Good News

Here's the part that surprises most buyers.

None of these costs should catch you off guard.

A great Realtor and lender will walk you through everything before you're writing checks or signing paperwork.

No confusing jargon.

No financial jump scares.

Just a clear plan so you know exactly what to expect.
