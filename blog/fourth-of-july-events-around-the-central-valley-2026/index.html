Fourth of July Events Around the Central Valley 2026

Looking for something fun to do this Fourth of July? Whether you're hoping to catch an incredible fireworks show, cheer on a hometown parade, or spend the day making memories with family and friends, there's no shortage of celebrations happening across the Central Valley.

We've rounded up some of the best Independence Day events happening throughout our local communities so you can start planning your holiday weekend.

🇺🇸 Modesto Independence Day Parade

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕘 9:30 AM 📍 Downtown Modesto (Begins at 10th Street & F Street)

A Modesto tradition since 1874, this patriotic parade features marching bands, decorated floats, classic cars, veterans, community organizations, and plenty of hometown pride. It's one of the oldest Fourth of July parades in California and a great way to kick off the holiday.

🎆 Don Pedro Lake Fireworks Show

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕤 Fireworks at 9:30 PM 📍 Fleming Meadows Recreation Area, La Grange

Spend the day boating, swimming, fishing, or picnicking before enjoying a spectacular fireworks display over Don Pedro Lake.

🇺🇸 Atwater Fourth of July Celebration

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕖 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM 📍 Ralston Park, 1550 Third Street, Atwater

This all day celebration includes a parade, live entertainment, local vendors, food, family activities, and an evening fireworks show.

🎇 Stockton Fourth of July Parade & Fireworks Festival

📅 Saturday, July 4

  • Parade:

10:00 AM

  • Festival:

5:00 PM

  • Fireworks:

9:30 PM

📍 Weber Point Events Center, Stockton

Celebrate with live music, food vendors, family activities, and one of the area's biggest waterfront fireworks displays.

🌊 Lodi Lake Fourth of July Celebration

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕓 Gates Open at 4:00 PM 📍 Lodi Lake Park, 1101 W. Turner Road, Lodi

Enjoy a relaxing afternoon at Lodi Lake before heading out to nearby community fireworks celebrations later that evening.

🚲 Riverbank Fourth of July Bicycle Parade

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕘 9:00 AM 📍 Plaza del Rio, Riverbank

Decorate your bicycle, scooter, wagon, or stroller in patriotic colors and join this fun community tradition that's perfect for families.

🎆 Manteca Fourth of July Celebration

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕤 Fireworks at 9:30 PM 📍 Big League Dreams Sports Park, Manteca

Bring your lawn chairs and enjoy a family friendly evening featuring food vendors, entertainment, children's activities, and a fireworks finale.

🎉 Turlock Independence Celebration

📅 Friday, July 3 🕕 6:00 PM

📅 Saturday, July 4 🕗 8:00 AM

📍 Stanislaus County Fairgrounds, Turlock

Celebrate with local vendors, food, live music, community activities, and patriotic festivities throughout the holiday weekend.

Have a Safe and Happy Fourth of July!
