Mid Year Real Estate Recap: Thank You for an Incredible First Half of 2026

As we reach the halfway point of 2026, I can't help but reflect on how grateful I am for every family, client, friend, and referral that has been part of this journey. Real estate is about so much more than buying and selling homes. Behind every transaction is a family starting a new chapter, achieving a goal, or making an important life change. Being trusted to guide people through those moments is something I never take for granted.

\### By the Numbers

The first half of 2026 has been an incredible one for Taylor Thompson Home Group.

Here's what we've accomplished together:

\ 40 families served \ 25 buyers helped find their new home \ 15 successful home sales \ More than \\$21.7 million\\ in closed and pending sales \ Over \\$146,000\\ negotiated in buyer credits and savings \ 10 active and coming soon listings across the Central Valley

While I'm incredibly proud of these numbers, what means the most are the relationships behind them.

Every Home Has a Story

Each transaction represents something unique.

A growing family needing more space.

A first-time buyer achieving the dream of homeownership.

A seller preparing for their next adventure.

An investor building long-term wealth.

Every closing reminds me why I love serving our Central Valley communities.

More Than Just Real Estate

One of the things I'm most proud of isn't found in the sales numbers.

Throughout the year we've continued investing in our community by hosting client appreciation events, family photo sessions, vision board parties, and opportunities to stay connected long after closing day.

To me, buying or selling a home is just the beginning of the relationship.

Our goal has always been to build a business centered on people, not transactions.

Thank You for Your Trust

Whether you've purchased a home with us, sold your property, attended one of our events, referred a friend, shared one of our social media posts, or simply followed along, thank you.

Your trust and support have allowed our business to continue growing, and we truly don't take that for granted.

The biggest compliment we can ever receive is when someone recommends us to a friend or family member.

Those referrals mean the world to us.

Looking Ahead to the Second Half of 2026
