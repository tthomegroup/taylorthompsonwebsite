Ranked #29 in Stanislaus and San Joaquin Counties

Grateful for the Trust Behind the Numbers

This year, I am incredibly honored to be ranked #29 among the Top 100 real estate agents in Stanislaus County and San Joaquin County based on 2025 sales volume.

With over 9,000 licensed agents across these two counties, this recognition is not something I take lightly. It represents more than numbers. It reflects relationships, trust, consistency, and the opportunity to guide families through one of the most important decisions of their lives.

Serving buyers and sellers throughout Modesto, Escalon, Turlock, and surrounding Central Valley communities continues to be both a responsibility and a privilege.

More Than Production. It Is Personal.

Real estate is not just contracts and closing dates. It is late night calls, strategy conversations, problem solving, negotiations, and showing up fully for people when they need clarity and confidence.

This recognition belongs just as much to the people who trust me as it does to me.

To my family, you are my why. Your patience, encouragement, and support allow me to serve at a high level. Every missed dinner, every quick call I had to step away for, every moment you gave me grace means more than I can ever fully express.

To my clients, friends, and referral partners, thank you for trusting me with your home buying and selling decisions. Your referrals, loyalty, and continued belief in what I do truly mean everything.

Why Experience Matters in Today’s Market

The real estate market in Stanislaus County and San Joaquin County continues to evolve. Strategy matters. Negotiation matters. Local knowledge matters.

Being active, staying informed, and guiding clients with clarity is what allows strong results to happen even in shifting markets. Rankings are not the goal. Serving well is.

And when you serve well consistently, the results follow.

Looking Ahead to 2026

I remain committed to letting God lead the way, trusting His timing, and continuing to serve with integrity and intention. I am grateful, motivated, and excited for what is ahead.

If you are thinking about buying a home in Modesto, selling in Stanislaus County, or exploring options in San Joaquin County, I would be honored to help guide you through the process.
