VERY IMPORTANT:
Your First Visit Matters.

If you visit a builder without your Realtor or without registering us on your first visit, the builder may refuse to allow representation later. That means you could lose access to professional guidance, negotiation help, and support throughout the transaction.

Before visiting ANY builder model home, reach out to us first. We will either accompany you in person or make sure you are properly registered with the builder ahead of time.
